Gate fixtures for the V8-chase program (preserved 2026-10-15, P1-S0.0)
======================================================================

These three files are the framework workloads used by every performance
gate in docs/v8-chase-p1-plan-2026-10-15.txt and by the Pilot A/B/R1
measurements in docs/specialization-pilot-2026-10-15.txt. They were
previously kept only in the gitignored benchmark-results/ tree; the
workspace copies were lost and these were recovered from
/tmp/trust-js-review-controls-wnym0ybo/ (file dates 2026-09-29, matching
the originals). Do not edit them: gate comparability depends on byte
identity. Any replacement must be recorded here with new hashes and a
re-baseline.

Timer change (2026-11-10, P3 stage 2; owner-approved; docs/v8-chase-p3-plan-2026-11-08.txt,
"The fixtures' timer"):
  The timed section's lines only: the fixtures read performance.now() when the global has one
  (Node, d8, JSC and the engine shell, which gained it the same day) and Date.now() otherwise,
  and report elapsed_ms rounded to 0.01 ms, as the call microbenchmarks do. Date.now()'s whole
  milliseconds were coarser than the 2% regression bar on React's ~30 ms pass. Every line before
  the first timer line is byte-identical to the originals, so copies cut there (the "warm"
  copies, which omit the timed pass) are unchanged; the workloads and their expected output
  fields are unchanged. Re-baseline: elapsed_ms from before the change compares only within its
  own session; instruction counts of the timed pass change by the timer's calls.
