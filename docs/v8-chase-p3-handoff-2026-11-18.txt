## Words the plan uses

- First stage, second stage: the template JIT's two compiles of a function. The first stage is its
  first native code, which fills the feedback (property, call and element caches). The second
  stage is a recompile of a hot function on settled feedback.
- The splice: how the second stage inlines. The caller is recompiled to bytecode from its AST with
  its callees' bodies spliced in behind identity guards, so inlined callees have no frames of
  their own.
- The tier: the speculative optimizing tier, crates/trust-js/src/opt/. It builds a graph from a
  function's bytecode and a snapshot of its feedback, on a compile thread, and its code replaces
  the second stage's. TRUST_JS_OPT=1 turns it on. "The tier off" is the default configuration.
- Splice and graph: the tier's two inlining modes (TRUST_JS_OPT_INLINE). At splice the tier
  compiles the spliced bytecode. At graph it inlines callees in its own graph, each with its
  frame states, as the exemplars do. "The flip" made graph the default on 2026-11-19; splice
  stays selectable, and it is still what the second stage does with the tier off.
- Exit, discard: a check that fails in the tier's code leaves it for the first stage's code or the
  VM (other engines' deoptimization). Code whose exits reach a limit (100, doubling with each
  recompile) is discarded and the function compiled again on what the exits taught.
- Fixtures: benchmarks/fixtures/{vue,react,djot}.js. Their "measured pass" is the fixture's own
  timed work; "steady state" is that work repeated in one process.
- Fixed runs: an Octane benchmark run a set number of times (5, 200) under a driver, for
  instruction counts; Octane's own runner is used for timed scores.
- node-mg, node-sp: Node limited to Maglev (`--max-opt=2`) and to Sparkplug (`--max-opt=1`).
- The exemplars: V8, SpiderMonkey and JSC, whose current source grounds every design choice.
- The owner decides forks and exits. The coordinator reviews, measures again, lands and writes the
  plan's records; so far that has been one person's assistant session directing implementers,
  each on a branch of its own.

## How the work is done

- A design record before code. Each stage and each step that makes a choice has a note in the plan
  with its exit; a fork goes to the owner with a recommendation grounded in what V8, SpiderMonkey
  and JSC do in their current source, with file:line citations and whether their reason applies to
  TRust (AGENTS.md, "Design decisions").
- Count first. A step starts from a census or a probe that says how many executions it reaches in
  each workload; the note states the bound; the record compares the measured gain with it. A change
  with no measured effect does not land.
- One line of work, one worktree, one evidence directory. The implementer works on a branch in its
  own git worktree, keeps logs and outputs in an evidence directory beside it (never /tmp, which is
  memory-backed and cleared at restart), with a dated STATUS.txt and a final report that marks each
  statement verified (by a named run) or inferred.
- The coordinator reviews the diff, reruns the decisive measurements on its own builds (fat LTO,
  the recount at each mode, the verifier sweep, cycles, the tests), fast-forwards main, and writes
  the record into the plan from the report. Records state what was measured, by whom, and what was
  left open.
- Tests are diagnostics (AGENTS.md, "Testing"): the nearest tests and the tier's modules per step;
  the whole library suite when a switch changes; full gates (Test262, difftest, the forced-exit and
