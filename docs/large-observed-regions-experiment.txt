Large observed native regions: opt-in admission experiment

TRUST_JS_OPT_JIT_LARGE_REGIONS=1 additionally requires TRUST_JS_OPT_JIT=hot,
TRUST_JS_OPT_JIT_COVERAGE=1 and TRUST_JS_OPT_JIT_OBSERVED_REGIONS=1. It does not
change the established <=256-bytecode policy when disabled. It admits only
ordinary original bodies257..4096 operations with an exact frozen positive
entry and at least one original-StackPlan-reachable unseen block-entry cut.
Unknown and missing feedback authorize no omission. Full original CFG/stack/
frame/value/range analyses, all handler/completion targets, slots and globally
unsupported suspension/disposal/fragment scans remain authoritative. No OSR
or active-frame transfer is added. Ordinary cuts use existing before-effect
same-invocation VM recovery; specialization misses and eight-miss retirement
remain separate. A first checkpoint snapshot may require frequent VM exits
on paths activated later; union bits provide no path frequency.

Independent-root generic own-data proofs start empty at every original block,
retain every observer/rebind within the chosen native block, and never import
facts over CFG edges. Original blocks are bounded4097; aggregate4096 events,
64 facts/block and262144 work remain the existing heap solver budgets. Quota
or proof failure declines the candidate; it never removes an unproven guard.

Large benefit is deliberately named observed_native_opportunity_heuristic,
not minimum saved tests. It sums guarded numeric and structural opportunities
on original normal-edge native reachability from entry, stops at every VM cut,
and gives handler roots no independent opportunity credit. Mutually exclusive
branches may overcount per invocation; Entered is a conservative fusion union,
not frequency or proof of completed normal execution. Every numeric entry and
field guard is debited, even an omitted numeric field site. Large Object-result
guard planning remains outside the existing256-operation Object proof bound.

Policy work includes40 units per selected reachable native opcode, native name
resolution allowance, all original-PC labels, full-source frame matrices and
slots, every cut's exact dirty-slice/known-owning-suffix publication, handler
dispatch and potential imported calls. Request work respects LOCAL_EFFECTS;
prepared work can differ because its settled integer-scalar FramePlan differs.
These are estimates, not actual IR. Diagnostic records expose prepared work.
The estimate conservatively charges every original-PC label even when certified
cold-block interiors need no physical native label. Their entry cuts and every
semantic branch, exception, completion and VM recovery destination remain intact.
Actual IR remains bounded16384 and65536 blocks before publication, including
post-sealing growth. Background workers still own only Send compiler state;
main-thread permits/frame/code leases/weak dependency pins are unchanged.

are provisional opt-in engineering policy. The older1024 coefficient had a
measured40IR/op/~5.9us codegen/IR rationale; reducing it is not evidence that
compilation repays. Uninstrumented release compile costs, exact native/cut

revision. No default/promotion or performance advantage is claimed here.
